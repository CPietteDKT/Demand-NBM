# SAP Scheduling Agreement POC — Standard Fit/Gap

## Why

The Digital team proposes an exploratory POC to assess a Wholesale commitment process using SAP contracts and Scheduling Agreements. Starting with full standard SAP is intended to reveal the ERP's capabilities and measure process fit and gaps before a target solution is chosen. This is a proposal, not an approved target process; it is a separate initiative from the Business's Wholesale Demand & Inventory work on PO flags.

## Capabilities

- **CAP-1**
  - **intent:** Assess whether the proposed contract and Scheduling Agreement process can be demonstrated with standard SAP.
  - **success:** A step-level fit/gap assessment records where the proposed process is supported by SAP standard and where gaps remain.

## Constraints

- Use full standard SAP as the baseline for the first assessment; do not presume custom development before standard capabilities have been assessed.

## Non-goals

- Approving a final target process or committing to a production solution based on the proposal alone.
- Designing or implementing custom SAP extensions as part of the standard-first assessment.
- Including or replacing the separate Business initiative for Wholesale Demand & Inventory and PO-flag handling.

## Success signal

The POC documents which steps in the proposed contract and Scheduling Agreement flow fit SAP standard and which gaps remain, giving stakeholders evidence to decide what to investigate or design next.

## Assumptions

- The process details, Resell-only scope, pilot criteria, named participants, and dates in the source are proposal inputs, not approved decisions.
- The proposal is tracked as the SAP Scheduling Agreement POC initiative; no dependency on the separate PO-flag initiative is assumed.
