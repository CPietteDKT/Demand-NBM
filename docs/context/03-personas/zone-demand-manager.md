# Persona — Zone Demand Manager (ZDM)

> **Source**: Responsibilities sheet "Zone Demand Manager", version Dec 2025.

## Mission
Develop and empower a team of Zone Merch Planner Managers to drive profitable growth, guaranteeing optimal product availability and inventory efficiency across the complete supply chain. **Strategic bridge between Offer, Supply, Retail and Finance** for the zone.

## Result KPIs
Overall DTB score · Succession planning · Teammates promoted · Value of FG & components destroyed · End-of-life product share · Turnover growth · Shortage rate · DSI · Margin/stock · Supply chain P&L performance · Supply margin (€) · Store margin (%).

## Responsibilities

1. **Management** — develop human capital; organisation, recruitment (9-box), development plans, compensation, compliance.
2. **Build and manage the zone demand strategy**
   - Aligned with commercial trajectory, procurement capacities and dispatch realities of the supply zone.
   - Animate ZMMs in translating it into perimeter strategies.
   - Ensure consistency across horizons (tactical, pre-season, in-season) and raise alerts.
3. **Financial and business performance**
   - Oversee supply chain milestones.
   - Lead the alignment of **Business Plan and forecast trajectory (Y+1 Sales & Purchase validation)** — checkpoint between commercial ambition and supply feasibility.
   - Set and achieve KPI targets; run supply chain diagnostics.
   - Accountable for the **Supply P&L** (purchase price PRMP, CPD, mark-up) — except Europe Supply Zone.
   - Monitor stock health: DSI milestones, EOL reduction, production → warehouse → store flow.
4. **Lead the transformation of supply processes and organisations** — continuous improvement; network animation standardising ways of working across countries.
5. **S&OP / S&OE animation** — prepare and participate in the Zone S&OP; propose prioritisation; guarantee demand/supply/retail alignment; advise on critical decisions in case of demand fluctuation or shortage.

## Means KPIs
Communicated zone demand strategy · S&OE meetings · Demand coherency across horizons · Milestone calendar · Piloting accuracy · Sales forecast accuracy · Supply performance · P&L management · Zone S&OP · Global demand planning consolidation.

## Relevance for the group
**Executive sponsor-level persona**: S&OP owner at zone level, arbitrates between commercial ambition and supply feasibility — the natural owner of cross-business-model trade-offs.
