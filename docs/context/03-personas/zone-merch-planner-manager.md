# Persona — Zone Merchandise Planner Manager (ZMM)

> **Source**: Job description "Zone Merch Planner Manager – 2026 – SRS.ZMM". Job group: Supply Chain; job family: Supply Retail & Sport.
> **Speciality**: Supply Zone.

## Mission
Develop and empower a team of Zone Merchandise Planners focused on profitable growth, guaranteeing optimal product availability and inventory across the supply chain.

## Result KPIs
SMR employee engagement rate · Value of FG & components destroyed · End-of-life product share · Turnover growth · Shortage rate · DSI · Margin/stock · Supply chain P&L performance · Supply margin (€) · Store margin (%).

## Responsibilities

1. **Managerial** — develop and take care of human capital; target organisation, recruitment, skills, compensation, change leadership, compliance.
2. **Build and manage the demand strategy for my area**
   - Write the demand strategy in line with the zone project plan; share it with stakeholders.
   - Support ZMPs in translating it into operational tactics.
   - Give macro inputs to ZDP for dispatch tactics.
   - As **S&OE manager**, guarantee demand/supply alignment for my universe.
3. **Improve demand planning excellence and agility**
   - Oversee supply chain milestones across horizons.
   - Align demand across horizons with all stakeholders.
   - Validate the universe business plan with Category Manager, Sports Director / Zone Category Development Manager, finance and sustainability.
   - Set and achieve KPI targets; promote profitable availability online and in store; run supply chain diagnostics.
4. **Lead the transformation of supply processes and organisations** — give meaning to process/tool changes; continuous improvement; conditions for success.
5. *(Optional)* Upskill the network (trainer / referent / diagnosis leader).
6. *(Optional, base camp)* Global demand planning consolidation and S&OP.

## Means KPIs
Communicated demand strategy · S&OE meetings · Milestone calendar · Demand planning coherence (piloting accuracy, sales forecast accuracy) · Supply performance monitoring · Transformation topics.

## Relevance for the group
**Decision-maker and consolidator**: needs aggregated, coherent views across horizons and ZMP perimeters; sponsor of process/tool transformation in the zone.
