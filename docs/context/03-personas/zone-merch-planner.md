# Persona — Zone Merchandise Planner (ZMP)

> **Source**: Job description "Zone Merch Planner – 2026 – SRS.ZMP". Job group: Supply Chain; job family: Supply Retail & Sport.
> **Specialities**: Base-camp, Supply Zone, Country.

## Mission
Guarantee profitable growth in my area by ensuring the best offer is available with optimal stock levels across the supply chain.

## Result KPIs
Turnover growth · Shortage · DSI · Retail margin (%) · Supply margin (€) · End-of-life product share · Value of finished goods & components destroyed.

## Responsibilities

1. **Secure the alignment of the economic framework**
   - Validate the Business Plan (BP Y+3, family level) with the Category Manager and ZMM; write demand tactics aligned with the sport's demand strategy.
   - Build and validate the **range costing** (number of products per range level) with the Category Manager.
   - Initialise turnover and **stock piloting**; define target inventory (**stock norm**).
   - Validate the **sales & purchase summary** of the selected offer.
   - Pilot activity through demand reporting and action plans.
2. **Pilot and ensure sales forecast reliability across horizons**
   - Tactical: super-model level, 2-year horizon.
   - Pre-season: forecasts during RFQs; validate sales forecast of the selected range.
   - In-season: initiate, analyse, adjust until end of life.
   - Keep economic piloting and sales forecasting aligned.
3. **Ensure reliable purchase planning**
   - 2-year tactical purchase forecast per super-model, based on tactical sales forecast, **new business models forecast (wholesale, rental…)**, industrial capacity feedback and BP alignment.
   - RFQ purchase quantities (pre-season); validate next-season purchase forecasts.
   - In-season purchase parameters (**MRP**); implantation orders; communicate changes via S&OE; manage TOP/FLOP decisions.
4. **Manage pricing** — target contract price, markup policy, purchase price events, retail price change proposals (incl. CPD).
5. **Manage stock distribution parameters** — dispatch tactics with ZDP; logistics parameters (UOM); store stock parameters (minimum merchandising, IQ).
6. *(Optional)* Upskill the network (trainer / referent).
7. *(Optional, base camp)* Global demand planning consolidation and S&OP with the Industrial Universe.

## Means KPIs
Forecast reliability across horizons (Tactical / RFQ / Quantity Selection / Actualised) · Pre-season forecast reliability (Initial / Last version / Realised) · In-season 12-week forecast reliability · Tactical demand planning (PQZD) · Purchase reliability · Implantation availability · Supply margin · CPD piloting · Referencing data · End-of-life rate.

## Key hard skills
Sales forecast management · Purchase forecast management · Economic framework management (+ S&OP/S&OE, pricing, demand tactics, inventory & distribution planning, Generative AI).

## Relevance for the group
Primary **daily user** of nearly every product in the group. Note: the job description already mentions **new business model forecasts (wholesale, rental)** as an input to tactical purchase planning.
