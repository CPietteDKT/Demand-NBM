# Demand Digital Product Group — Vision

> **Vision statement**
> *"Enable Sales & Operations Planning by aligning multi-dimensional forecast and inventory planning through a unified experience to optimise inbound flow management."*

## Reading the vision

| Keyword | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| **Sales & Operations Planning (S&OP)** | The group serves the S&OP / S&OE process end to end, from 3-year business plan to in-season replenishment. |
| **Multi-dimensional forecast** | Forecasts across horizons (tactical Y+2/Y+3, pre-season, in-season), granularities (family, super-model, model/item, store) and — potentially — business models. |
| **Inventory planning** | Target stock levels (stock norm), stock piloting, replenishment parameters. |
| **Unified experience** | A coherent journey across the group's products for planners and managers. |
| **Inbound flow management** | The ultimate output: the right purchase/order quantities (MRP) flowing into the network. |

## Current business context

Decathlon relies mainly on an **integrated retailer business model**; its supply chain — and therefore the group's products — are designed for this single model. The One by 30 project calls for diversification through **new business models**, some already addressed today but **not in an industrialised or scalable way**.
