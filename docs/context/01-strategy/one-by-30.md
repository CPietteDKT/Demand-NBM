# One by 30 — Decathlon Company Project

> **Classification**: Strictly Confidential — Internal Use Only.
> **Source**: "One by 30" internal presentation (French), translated and condensed.

## 1. Our DNA

### Values
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Vitality** | Positive energy; dare, act, adapt; play to win, not to avoid losing. |
| **Generosity** | Team play, help and share; treat all stakeholders as friends; care for oneself and others. |
| **Responsibility** | Say what you do, do what you say; measure value created; fix mistakes fast; make all stakeholders durably win. |
| **Authenticity** | Be true to oneself and others; speak simply; act sincerely; respect nature and its resources. |

### Intentions ("Volontés")
1. **People at the heart** — autonomous and responsible teams; every day is a new starting line; balance robustness and agility; share value creation.
2. **Value-for-money positioning** — lower costs while respecting people and planet; **simplify the company**.
3. **Technology & innovation leadership** — technology serving people; innovations that improve users' lives.
4. **Planet & environment** — limit negative impact; move towards a regenerative economy.
5. **Sport & performance** — encourage people to move; invest in expertise, style and performance at the best value/price.
6. **Have fun** — rigorous in sport, joyful day-to-day; celebrate wins.

### Purpose
- **Make wellbeing accessible to all** — while consolidating the sport core business, extend legitimacy to overall wellbeing (recovery, nutrition, sleep, mental, sport experiences). "Wellbeing is Decathlon's new CRM."
- **Bring people together through sport** — create sport communities, recruit and retain new sportspeople; the Decathlon Foundation helps those without access.
- Tagline: *Together through sport, wellbeing for all.*

## 2. Vision 2035

- **Positive impact on 2+ billion people** and **10% market share** by 2035.
- Two singular strengths amplified:
  1. **Local human connection** — teammates as the beating heart; millions of local sport communities; interactions hyper-personalised with AI; a "Vitality index".
  2. **Accessible & unique sport solutions** — products, services and experiences; seamless personalised omnichannel; service platforms (repair, **rental**, coaching, wellbeing, community); **on-demand production and reinforced circular models** for an always-available offer; an ecosystem of **partners and diverse models** to reach every corner of the world.
- Promises to: Teammates, Customers, Users, Planet & Society, Partners, Shareholders — with sustainable business models as the engine of revenue.

## 3. Project 2026-2030

Structure: **4 Growth Engines, 2 Enablers, 1 Foundation**.

| Type | Pillar | Key ambitions |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | **Engaged & Performing Teammates** | Culture of meaning, responsibility and passion; leadership, trust; upskilling incl. Digi-AI. |
| Enabler | **Radically Simple & Frugal** | Simplify and strengthen operations; restore competitiveness. |
| Enabler | **Digitally Augmented** | Simplify users' daily life through digital; trusted, flexible, future-proof platform; turn data insights into strategic assets for decisions. |
| Growth engine | **Omni Commerce Amplified** | Seamless omnichannel experience; acquisition & retention; **resilience and growth models**. |
| Growth engine | **Smart, Innovative & Circular Offer** | Exclusive products with unbeatable value/price; locally relevant offer selection. |
| Growth engine | **Responsive, Resilient & Responsible Operations** | **Responsive: demand-driven planning**; Resilient: efficient physical network; Responsible: sustainable and transparent operations. |
| Growth engine | **Disruptive Growth by Decathlon PULSE** | Develop and protect our footprint; innovate and explore to prepare the future. |

## 4. Relevance for the Demand Digital product group

- **Responsive Operations → demand-driven planning** is a direct mandate for the group.
- **Growth models, circular offer, rental/services, partners** imply demand and inventory planning beyond the integrated retail model.
- **Digitally Augmented** and **Radically Simple** frame expectations on a unified, data-driven, simple planning experience.
