# Business Models — Current State

> **Status**: Draft. Only facts stated by the product group are recorded as such; everything else is marked as hypothesis or open question.

## Facts

- Decathlon relies **essentially on an integrated retailer business model** (design, source, distribute and sell its own products through its own stores and e-commerce).
- The **supply chain and the Demand Digital products are oriented towards this single business model**.
- The **One by 30** project aims at diversification through **new business models**.
- Some new business models are **already addressed today, but not in an industrialised or scalable way**.
- The ZMP job description (2026) already lists **wholesale** and **rental** forecasts as inputs to tactical purchase planning.
- Vision 2035 mentions **rental, repair, circular models, on-demand production, partners and diverse models**.
